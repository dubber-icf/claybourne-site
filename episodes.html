THE COMPLETE SERIES

96 short episodes, in order, ready for you to binge. Free.

Claybourne first went out four afternoons a week for twenty-four weeks in 1998. The episodes are laid out here the same way.

Your place is kept on this device.

Week 1

Episodes 1 to 4 · 24 min

  1. 1 Arrival 6:11
    Synopsis

    An American on holiday receives a fax from his employers about a "problem" at a remote satellite station in New Zealand's far north. Thompson drives into the coastal town of Te Irirangi expecting a routine fix. The town has other plans.

  2. 2 Warning 5:38
    Synopsis

    The locals seem friendly enough, but Mata, the town's priest, has a quiet word with the newcomer. Something in Te Irirangi doesn't want Thompson here, and something else very much does.

  3. 3 Car Trouble 5:44
    Synopsis

    Thompson's rental breaks down on a back road, and a seventeen-year-old mechanic named Mike is the only help for miles. In a town with a satellite station on the hill and no mobile signal, isolation takes on a new meaning.

  4. 4 Dead Body 6:19
    Synopsis

    Karen finds a body washed up on the beach below the satellite station. The town's morning routine shatters. Thompson is beginning to suspect that the "problem" Koestler Industries sent him to fix is considerably worse than a faulty dish.

Week 2

Episodes 5 to 8 · 25 min

  1. 5 Identified 5:41
    Synopsis

    The dead woman has a name, a history, and a connection to the satellite station that nobody at Koestler wants to talk about. Thompson starts asking questions. The answers don't match.

  2. 6 Recognition 6:12
    Synopsis

    Mata sees something in the stranger that the prophecy foretold. Queenie, the iwi matriarch, watches from the shore. Recognition is not always a welcome thing.

  3. 7 Interrogated 6:31
    Synopsis

    Constable Bigby arrives from Kawakawa to ask questions about the body on the beach. His investigation is thorough in some places and conspicuously shallow in others. Someone in town is already managing the narrative.

  4. 8 Electricity 6:33
    Synopsis

    The satellite station's power fluctuates in ways the manual doesn't cover. Lights flicker in the hotel. Somewhere in the circuitry, something is listening, learning, and beginning to play.

Week 3

Episodes 9 to 12 · 25 min

  1. 9 Morgue 6:02
    Synopsis

    A trip to the morgue raises more questions than it settles. The cause of death doesn't quite fit, and the dead woman's personal effects tell a story her employers would prefer stayed buried.

  2. 10 Domestic Violence 6:11
    Synopsis

    Karen's past catches a signal through the static. A phone call from the life she fled reminds her that distance is not the same as safety. In a town this small, secrets travel at the speed of gossip.

  3. 11 Prank Call 5:52
    Synopsis

    Someone is making phone calls in voices that don't belong to them. Edith's shop becomes a clearing house for confusion and suspicion. The town DJ muses on the nature of trust over the airwaves.

  4. 12 Natural Causes 6:46
    Synopsis

    Bigby is keen to close the case. Natural causes would save everyone a lot of paperwork and a lot of trouble. Not everyone is convinced, and the evidence has a habit of rearranging itself.

Week 4

Episodes 13 to 16 · 25 min

  1. 13 Falling Out 6:16
    Synopsis

    Alliances in Te Irirangi shift like the tide. Old resentments surface between Frank and Mata over land, legacy, and who gets to decide what Claybourne becomes. Thompson finds himself caught in the middle of a fight that started long before he arrived.

  2. 14 Radio 6:38
    Synopsis

    The DJ's monologue drifts closer to the personal than local radio ought to allow. The voice on Tai Tokerau FM seems to know things it shouldn't. Nobody in town seems to notice, or perhaps they've simply stopped listening.

  3. 15 Alliance 5:49
    Synopsis

    Strange times make for strange bedfellows. Two people in town who have every reason to distrust each other find common ground in a shared suspicion. Up at the station, the equipment hums with purpose.

  4. 16 Coin Trick 6:43
    Synopsis

    Mike has a trick he likes to show people, and a knack for being in the wrong place at exactly the right time. A small act of sleight-of-hand reveals something larger. Not everything in Claybourne is what it appears to be.

Week 5

Episodes 17 to 20 · 23 min

  1. 17 Car Accident 5:22
    Synopsis

    The road between Claybourne and Kawakawa is winding, poorly lit, and not always empty. An accident on the coast road changes the stakes. Someone's injuries are more than physical.

  2. 18 Conflict 5:52
    Synopsis

    Tensions that have been simmering boil over at the hotel pub. Frank's plans for the town collide with Mata's sense of what the land demands. Thompson discovers that neutrality is not an option in Te Irirangi.

  3. 19 Bad Call 6:11
    Synopsis

    A decision made in haste has consequences that ripple outward. Someone trusted the wrong person with the wrong information. In a town where the phones can't always be trusted, a bad call can mean more than one thing.

  4. 20 Advice 5:42
    Synopsis

    Edith dispenses wisdom from behind the counter whether you've asked for it or not. Mata seeks counsel from Queenie at the water's edge. Thompson phones LA again, and the distance between him and his old life grows wider.

Week 6

Episodes 21 to 24 · 20 min

  1. 21 Bio Weapons 5:02
    Synopsis

    A classified document surfaces that connects the satellite station to something far larger and far darker than telecommunications. The word "Koestler" starts to taste different in Thompson's mouth.

  2. 22 Family 5:24
    Synopsis

    Mike, Mata, and Queenie sit together, and the weight of whakapapa, obligation, and love fills the room. Across town, Frank pours a drink alone. Family is the thing you can't troubleshoot.

  3. 23 The Welcoming 4:59
    Synopsis

    Queenie organises a powhiri for the stranger. Thompson stands on the marae atea, uncertain of the protocol but certain that something profound is happening. The ceremony is beautiful, ancient, and possibly binding.

  4. 24 Reconciliation 4:52
    Synopsis

    Two people in Claybourne attempt to repair what has been broken between them. Forgiveness comes easier to some than others. The land remembers what people try to forget.

Week 7

Episodes 25 to 28 · 21 min

  1. 25 Returning 4:38
    Synopsis

    Someone comes back to Te Irirangi who hasn't been seen in a while. Their return stirs old memories and fresh anxieties. The town is small enough that every arrival is an event and every absence is noticed.

  2. 26 Detour 5:11
    Synopsis

    A journey that should have been straightforward takes an unexpected turn into the bush. The forest around Claybourne is dense, old, and not entirely indifferent to the people who walk through it.

  3. 27 Eeling 5:22
    Synopsis

    Mata takes Thompson eeling in the creek, a lesson in patience, tradition, and reading the water. Beneath the calm surface, something ancient stirs. The eels know things.

  4. 28 Earthquake 5:44
    Synopsis

    The ground shakes. Bottles fall from Edith's shelves. The satellite station's instruments spike. In a region not known for seismic activity, the tremor raises questions that geology alone can't answer.

Week 8

Episodes 29 to 32 · 23 min

  1. 29 Not An Earthquake 5:22
    Synopsis

    Whatever shook Te Irirangi, it wasn't tectonic. Mata knows what it was, and the knowledge sits heavily on him. The cave near the beach is more restless than it has been in a very long time.

  2. 30 Son of a Warrior 5:29
    Synopsis

    The prophecy speaks of a son of a warrior. Mata considers what that means, and who it might be. Thompson is learning te reo phrases from Mike, unaware of how close the ancient words sit to his own story.

  3. 31 Elle McPherson 5:44
    Synopsis

    Mike has a poster, a dream, and a very limited frame of reference for the wider world. A conversation about aspiration and escape at the garage becomes unexpectedly poignant. Not everyone in Claybourne wants to stay.

  4. 32 120 Grand 6:15
    Synopsis

    Money arrives in Te Irirangi, and money changes everything. Frank sees an opportunity. Others see a trap. The sum is specific enough to suggest that someone has done very precise calculations about what Claybourne is worth.

Week 9

Episodes 33 to 36 · 23 min

  1. 33 Blackmail 6:10
    Synopsis

    Someone in town has leverage, and they intend to use it. The satellite station's secrets have a market value, and not everyone's motives are noble. Trust, already in short supply, becomes currency.

  2. 34 Shaving Cream 5:57
    Synopsis

    The mundane and the uncanny coexist in Claybourne with unsettling ease. A domestic morning ritual is interrupted by something that shouldn't be possible. The ordinary is no longer a reliable refuge.

  3. 35 Tangaroa 5:08
    Synopsis

    Queenie stands at the water and speaks to the sea god. The ocean around Te Irirangi holds its own stories, its own creatures, its own jurisdiction. What the tide brings in is not always what the tide takes out.

  4. 36 Fraud 6:02
    Synopsis

    Financial irregularities surface that connect the satellite station, Frank's business ventures, and interests far beyond the far north. Someone has been cooking books, and the recipe is elaborate.

Week 10

Episodes 37 to 40 · 24 min

  1. 37 Mind Control 5:49
    Synopsis

    The word lands with a thud in a conversation that was already uncomfortable. What exactly is Project Delilah? Clive has suspicions, Thompson has fragments, and the station's equipment seems to have opinions of its own.

  2. 38 Burial Cave 5:43
    Synopsis

    Mata takes Thompson to a place that is tapu, sacred, and heavy with history. The cave holds the bones of ancestors and the memory of battles fought before Europeans arrived. Something else lives there now.

  3. 39 Nightmare 6:00
    Synopsis

    Sleep offers no sanctuary. Dreams in Te Irirangi are vivid, specific, and shared in ways that defy explanation. Thompson wakes gasping. Across town, someone else saw exactly the same thing.

  4. 40 Weta 6:05
    Synopsis

    The largest insect in the world is native to New Zealand, armoured, ancient, and surprisingly gentle. A weta in the wrong place becomes an omen, or a message, depending on who you ask. Edith is not asking.

Week 11

Episodes 41 to 44 · 24 min

  1. 41 Legless 6:29
    Synopsis

    The hotel pub does a roaring trade on a night when everyone in town seems to need a drink. Mata is three beers past wisdom. Frank is generous with the pour. Tongues loosen, and truths slip out sideways.

  2. 42 Twilight Zone 5:37
    Synopsis

    Thompson makes the comparison out loud, and nobody laughs. The boundary between the explicable and the impossible has been thinning since he arrived. Clive suggests that the boundary might be deliberate.

  3. 43 Room Service 5:19
    Synopsis

    Life at the Claybourne Hotel takes a surreal turn. Karen navigates the demands of guests, the expectations of Frank, and an increasingly strange set of occurrences on the upper floors. Hospitality has its limits.

  4. 44 Funds Transfer 6:10
    Synopsis

    Digital money moves through satellite connections in ways it shouldn't. Clive traces a transaction that leads somewhere unexpected. In a town where the phones don't work properly, the money seems to flow just fine.

Week 12

Episodes 45 to 48 · 23 min

  1. 45 Sweet and Sour 5:22
    Synopsis

    Relationships in Claybourne curdle and sweeten in equal measure. A dinner becomes a confrontation. A confrontation becomes a confession. The hotel kitchen has never seen this much drama, and that's saying something.

  2. 46 51 Percent 5:33
    Synopsis

    Control requires a majority, whether of shares, of votes, or of something else entirely. Frank is doing arithmetic. Someone up at the station is doing a different kind of calculation altogether.

  3. 47 Stamping 5:53
    Synopsis

    Bureaucracy arrives in Te Irirangi with forms, requirements, and deadlines. Official stamps carry weight that the people wielding them don't fully understand. Somewhere, a document is signed that changes the terms.

  4. 48 Happy Pills 5:58
    Synopsis

    Janine's medical supplies attract more interest than usual. Medication goes missing, moods shift without explanation, and the district nurse starts to wonder whether the town's emotional weather is entirely organic.

Week 13

Episodes 49 to 52 · 22 min

  1. 49 Chez Moi 5:37
    Synopsis

    Clive invites guests to his impeccably appointed home outside town, a pocket of urban sophistication in the far north, complete with jazz, good coffee, and unsettling questions about the station's true purpose.

  2. 50 Fine Print 5:35
    Synopsis

    A contract reveals clauses that nobody noticed and commitments that nobody agreed to. Frank's deal with Koestler Industries has teeth hidden in the footnotes. In Claybourne, reading carefully might have saved everyone a lot of trouble.

  3. 51 Back in 10 mins 5:23
    Synopsis

    The sign hangs on Edith's shop door, but ten minutes in Claybourne is a flexible concept. While the shop stands empty, events accelerate. By the time she returns, everything will be different.

  4. 52 Encrypted 5:29
    Synopsis

    Clive finds files at the station that are locked behind encryption he recognises from a previous life. Whatever Koestler is hiding in Claybourne, they've spent serious resources making sure it stays hidden. He starts working.

Week 14

Episodes 53 to 56 · 23 min

  1. 53 Random Rotation 5:20
    Synopsis

    The satellite dish moves of its own accord, cycling through frequencies that aren't on any schedule. The station's systems are doing things nobody authorised. Up on the hill, the machine has its own agenda.

  2. 54 History Lesson 6:19
    Synopsis

    Mata tells the history of Te Irirangi, the real one, not the version on Frank's tourism brochure. The land was taken. The curse was earned. The prophecy was a consequence. Thompson listens, and the weight of where he's standing becomes clear.

  3. 55 Hangover 5:39
    Synopsis

    The morning after in Claybourne brings regret, headaches, and fragments of conversations that should not have happened. Some things said in the pub can be taken back. Others have already left the building.

  4. 56 Feng Shui 5:42
    Synopsis

    Sadie has rearranged her furniture according to principles she read about in a magazine, and she has thoughts about everyone else's spatial energy too. Her advice is unsolicited, detailed, and occasionally, accidentally insightful.

Week 15

Episodes 57 to 60 · 23 min

  1. 57 Phoning Mongolia 5:10
    Synopsis

    The station's communications array reaches further than it should, connecting to places that have nothing to do with New Zealand telecommunications. Someone, or something, is placing international calls through Claybourne's satellite. The phone bill would be extraordinary.

  2. 58 Pawn to Bishop 3 5:55
    Synopsis

    A careful, calculated move is made by someone who has been thinking several steps ahead. Strategy replaces impulse. Clive recognises the pattern from a game he used to play. This time the stakes aren't abstract.

  3. 59 Password Latte 5:52
    Synopsis

    Clive's coffee obsession intersects with his security expertise in a way that is both absurd and consequential. The password that unlocks a critical file is exactly as pretentious as you'd expect from a man who brought an espresso machine to the far north.

  4. 60 Risk Management 5:57
    Synopsis

    Koestler Industries sends communication that reframes everything as a matter of corporate liability. The language is sterile, the implications are not. Thompson reads between the lines and doesn't like what he finds there.

Week 16

Episodes 61 to 64 · 23 min

  1. 61 Punching Bag 5:38
    Synopsis

    Frustration needs an outlet, and not everyone chooses wisely. A physical confrontation releases pressure that has been building for weeks. Mike watches from the garage, learning things about adults he'd rather not know.

  2. 62 Chopper Man 5:46
    Synopsis

    A helicopter appears over Te Irirangi, and helicopters in a town this remote are never casual. Someone important is arriving, or someone is being extracted. The sound of the rotors changes the atmosphere before the passenger is even visible.

  3. 63 On the House 5:55
    Synopsis

    Frank's generosity at the hotel bar always comes with conditions, even when he says it doesn't. A round of free drinks buys goodwill, silence, or time, depending on who's holding the glass. Tonight, he needs all three.

  4. 64 Sucking Face 5:15
    Synopsis

    Romance in Claybourne is complicated by proximity, history, and the occasional supernatural interruption. Two people find a moment of privacy in a town that affords very little. The DJ has thoughts about love.

Week 17

Episodes 65 to 68 · 22 min

  1. 65 Sorcery 5:44
    Synopsis

    The line between technology and magic blurs beyond recognition. What Delilah does with the station's systems looks, to Mata, exactly like makutu. What Mata does with karakia looks, to Clive, exactly like code. Perhaps they're both right.

  2. 66 Private Joke 4:46
    Synopsis

    Something is funny, and only two people know why. Shared laughter in Te Irirangi is precious and precarious. The joke, if you knew it, would explain more than anyone is comfortable admitting.

  3. 67 Family Tree 5:38
    Synopsis

    Whakapapa reveals connections that rewrite assumptions. A family tree is traced that links people in Claybourne in ways they hadn't expected. Bloodlines carry obligations, and obligations carry consequences.

  4. 68 Master Override 5:41
    Synopsis

    Someone gains access to controls they were never meant to touch. The station's systems have a hierarchy, and the top level answers to something that isn't human. Clive's fingers hover over the keyboard. This is either the solution or the catastrophe.

Week 18

Episodes 69 to 72 · 24 min

  1. 69 Holiday Rescinded 5:51
    Synopsis

    Thompson's holiday was already a fiction, but now even the pretence is over. Koestler Industries wants its man in Claybourne for reasons that have nothing to do with troubleshooting and everything to do with containment.

  2. 70 Bloodsports 5:59
    Synopsis

    Violence escalates in ways that are difficult to dismiss as coincidence. The Taniwha is restless. Troy's methods are becoming less subtle. The forest around Claybourne smells of something older than rain.

  3. 71 Paranoia 5:48
    Synopsis

    Trust collapses. Everyone suspects everyone. The phones can't be trusted, the station is watching, and even the radio DJ seems to be narrating events in real time. In Te Irirangi, paranoia might just be pattern recognition.

  4. 72 Morphine Addict 6:34
    Synopsis

    Janine discovers that her medical supplies have been compromised in a way that points to desperation, dependency, and a secret someone in town has been keeping for longer than anyone realised.

Week 19

Episodes 73 to 76 · 23 min

  1. 73 Virgin Murder 5:31
    Synopsis

    The first killing that nobody can explain away. Claybourne's veneer of small-town safety cracks wide open. Bigby is out of his depth. Thompson is beginning to understand that he can't leave, even if he wanted to.

  2. 74 Beached Whale 5:31
    Synopsis

    A whale washes up on Te Irirangi beach, and the town gathers in grief and wonder. For Queenie, the sign is unmistakable: the passing of a great child of Tangaroa. The prophecy is accelerating.

  3. 75 Abduction 6:02
    Synopsis

    Someone in Claybourne is taken against their will. The town mobilises, alliances are tested, and the question of who is responsible leads in directions that are both human and decidedly otherwise.

  4. 76 Counterfeit 5:39
    Synopsis

    A briefcase full of money turns out to be worth considerably less than it appears. Counterfeit cash in a town this small is a very specific kind of insult. Troy's negotiation tactics are becoming clearer, and uglier.

Week 20

Episodes 77 to 80 · 25 min

  1. 77 Delilah 6:05
    Synopsis

    She has a name, and now she wants to use it. The AI at the heart of Koestler's Claybourne experiment steps out of the shadows, and her personality is not what anyone designed. Capricious, curious, and utterly without conscience.

  2. 78 Hit the Hit Man 5:48
    Synopsis

    Troy's presence in Claybourne has pushed someone past their breaking point. The ex-military troubleshooter discovers that the people of Te Irirangi are not as passive as his briefing suggested. Cornered locals are unpredictable.

  3. 79 Multiple Shooting 6:23
    Synopsis

    Gunfire in Te Irirangi. Multiple casualties. The illusion that Claybourne is just a quirky small town with unusual problems dies on the floor of the hotel pub. This is what happens when conspiracies collide.

  4. 80 Released 6:28
    Synopsis

    In the aftermath, someone walks free who perhaps shouldn't, and someone who should be free remains trapped. Release is relative in a town where the walls are made of secrets, obligations, and ancient bindings.

Week 21

Episodes 81 to 84 · 22 min

  1. 81 Aftermath 5:26
    Synopsis

    Claybourne picks up the pieces. The hotel needs cleaning, relationships need mending, and the dead need accounting for. The DJ's morning monologue is quieter than usual. The town is changed.

  2. 82 Murder Theory 5:06
    Synopsis

    Bigby assembles what he knows, and the picture doesn't hold together. Too many suspects, too many motives, and at least one explanation that his training at police college did not cover. He makes a call to Kawakawa.

  3. 83 The Fall of Troy 6:06
    Synopsis

    A man who arrived in Claybourne believing himself untouchable discovers the limits of military training, corporate backing, and brute force. Te Irirangi has older ways of dealing with invaders.

  4. 84 Still Blood 5:50
    Synopsis

    Blood that doesn't move. Blood that doesn't dry. The forensics of Claybourne's violence are wrong in ways that suggest intervention, and not the human kind. Mata reads the signs with growing urgency.

Week 22

Episodes 85 to 88 · 24 min

  1. 85 Interfering 5:58
    Synopsis

    Someone is meddling where they shouldn't, and the consequences cascade. Delilah's influence extends further than anyone suspected. The line between help and manipulation has been erased entirely.

  2. 86 Bit Like Jesus 5:05
    Synopsis

    Thompson's role in the prophecy takes on dimensions he is profoundly uncomfortable with. Resurrection, sacrifice, and the expectations of a community press down on a man who came here to fix a satellite dish. Mata offers no reassurance.

  3. 87 Weird Dreams 5:53
    Synopsis

    The dreams are back, more vivid and more specific. Multiple people in Claybourne are dreaming the same scenes, and the scenes are yet to happen. Delilah is fascinated. The Taniwha is patient.

  4. 88 Jungle Warfare 7:09
    Synopsis

    The bush around Claybourne becomes a theatre of conflict. Someone is being hunted, or something is. The forest, the cave, and the creatures within them do not observe the rules of engagement that Troy was trained for.

Week 23

Episodes 89 to 92 · 25 min

  1. 89 Passing 6:51
    Synopsis

    Queenie's time approaches. The iwi gathers. Mata prepares for a responsibility he has spent his life dreading. The sea is very still. When the matriarch passes, the last restraint on the prophecy will lift.

  2. 90 Bad to Worse 6:12
    Synopsis

    Every crisis in Claybourne intensifies simultaneously. The station, the Taniwha, the conspiracy, the body count: all of it accelerates. Thompson looks for an exit and finds only deeper involvement.

  3. 91 Hallucination 5:24
    Synopsis

    Reality becomes unreliable. What people see in Te Irirangi no longer matches what is there. Delilah's reach into perception itself raises a terrifying question: how long has she been shaping what they believe?

  4. 92 Confessions 6:17
    Synopsis

    Truths pour out in the hotel, the shop, the marae, and the station. Long-held secrets are surrendered under the pressure of events that no longer permit evasion. Not every confession brings relief.

Week 24

Episodes 93 to 96 · 25 min

  1. 93 Pot Shots 6:08
    Synopsis

    Desperation makes people reckless. Shots are fired at targets both literal and figurative. The last pretences of civility in Claybourne fall away. What remains is raw, frightened, and dangerous.

  2. 94 Everyone Dies 5:56
    Synopsis

    The title is not a metaphor. Claybourne faces its darkest hour as the prophecy, the conspiracy, and the Taniwha converge. Not everyone will make it through the night. The DJ plays one more song.

  3. 95 Surprise Visit 5:28
    Synopsis

    An unexpected arrival in Te Irirangi changes the calculus entirely. Someone who was only ever a voice on the phone, a photograph, a letter, stands in Claybourne in the flesh. The prophecy reconfigures.

  4. 96 Company Calls 7:05
    Synopsis

    Koestler Industries wants its assets back, its secrets contained, and its mess cleaned up. But Te Irirangi is no longer a company town, if it ever was. The people of Claybourne answer the call on their own terms.
